Who you are buying from

Six companies. One manufacturer.

Alcami is a Thai-owned enterprise made up of six companies operating four locations in three countries — Thailand, the Philippines and Singapore. We have designed and manufactured plastic molds, parts and casings since 2001.

In Thailand that means two plants: our flagship hub in Prachinburi, equipped for both cleanroom and industrial production, and our Rayong site on the Eastern Seaboard, home to Alcami Industries and Alcami Extrusion.

We work across electronics, healthcare, automotive and food — the industries where a supplier gets audited, and where being able to answer for every step of the process matters more than being the cheapest quote in the inbox.

FAQ

Manufacturing with Alcami, answered.

Still have a question about your part or project? Ask our team →

Which plastic manufacturing processes does Alcami run in-house?

Thermoforming and injection molding are our two production processes, and both are supported in-house: we extrude our own sheet, design and CNC-machine our own molds, then finish with DI washing, assembly, welding and testing. CNC machining, film conversion, warehousing and transport round out the group, so one supplier covers the part from raw material to delivered pallet.

Thermoforming or injection molding — which should I choose?

Injection molding suits small, precise parts at high volumes; thermoforming suits larger parts and faster, cheaper tooling. Because we run both, we recommend the process that genuinely fits your part rather than the one we happen to have free capacity on. Send the drawing and we will tell you which way we would go, and why.

Do you make your own tooling?

Yes. Mold design, CAM programming and CNC machining all happen on our own floor, on our own equipment, by our own engineers — including the CNC knife bending we use to make thermoforming blades. When a first article needs a correction it becomes a job on our shop floor rather than a purchase order to another country.

Which materials do you work with?

Standard grades include ABS, PS/HIPS, PP, HDPE, PET/APET, PC, POM, PA and PMMA, plus TPU for overmolded parts, and we extrude PS, PP, HDPE and ABS sheet ourselves. We also offer bio-based, biodegradable and recycled grades, and our engineers will suggest options that lower the environmental load of your project.

Are you certified, and can I have copies for my supplier file?

Yes. The group is certified to ISO 9001 for quality, ISO 14001 for environment and ISO 22000 for food safety, and cleanroom production and our own test lab sit behind those certificates rather than alongside them. Tell us what your qualification pack needs and we will send the certificate copies with it.

Can you manufacture medical, food-contact or electronics parts?

Yes. We mold and form in Class 10,000 / ISO 7 cleanrooms, wash parts with ultra-pure deionized water from our own DI plant, and can double-seal or vacuum-pack them for controlled environments. ISO 22000 covers food-contact work, and our in-house lab handles cleanliness and ESD testing for electronics.

What is your minimum order quantity?

MOQ is assessed per project. A promotional item, an electronics casing and a medical tray sit at very different volumes, and the process we would choose changes the answer again. Send the part and your target annual volume and we will quote against it.

Can you help with import duty and shipping from Thailand?

Yes. We can apply for FORM D, FORM E and FORM AI certificates of origin on your behalf so you can claim preferential import duty where your country qualifies, and we run our own warehousing and domestic transport, so parts arrive at your forwarder or your door rather than only at our loading bay.

How do I start, and how quickly will I hear back?

Send a 3D CAD file or a drawing with your material, tolerances and annual volume. We reply to quote requests within 1 business day, usually with a budgetary quotation or the questions we need answered to produce one.
